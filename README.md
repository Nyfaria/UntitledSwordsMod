Combat Oddities is a mod that adds 27 new swords to Minecraft! These swords aren't only visually pleasing; each one has one or more unique abilities that make using them fun and powerful.

These swords are not craftable. Instead, they can be bought from the Bladesmith (a new villager type) for 10-12 emeralds each. Players need to craft the Netherite Anvil and place it down near regular villagers in order to trade with a Bladesmith.

## Sword List

**Charged Greatblade**<br>
Grants fire resistance when held<br>
Shoots two fire charges<br>
Sets nearby foes ablaze when held<br>
Will not harm blazes<br>

**Glass Sword**<br>
Keeps glass intact when breaking it<br>
Fragile: 10% shatter on hit<br>

**Cinder Blade**<br>
1/3 chance to set targets ablaze<br>
Doubles as flint and steel<br>

**Midnight Enderblade**<br>
Deals bonus damage to ender mobs<br>
Use to hurl an ender pearl<br>

**Sword of Shards**<br>
Use to shoot a loose shard<br>

**Flame Zapper**<br>
Sets targets ablaze<br>
Charges creepers on hit<br>

**Oozing Blade**<br>
Rarely leaves lava where target stands<br>
Doubles as flint and steel<br>

**Corrosive Saber**<br>
Chance to poison targets<br>
Withers plants around struck foes<br>
Use to fire a corrosive bolt<br>

**Purple Axeblade**<br>
Doubles as an axe for stripping log<br>

**Galactic Triblade**<br>
Every third strike deals bonus damage<br>
Use to call down lightning where you aim<br>

**Gilded Fang**<br>
Poisons animals<br>
Will not harm undead mobs<br>

**Molten Sword**<br>
Grants fire resistance to player when held<br>
Use to launch a fire charge<br>

**Valentine Sword**<br>
Will not harm animals<br>
Interact with your pets to heal them<br>

**Charged Halo Sword**<br>
Grants flight when held<br>
Use to call down lightning where you aim<br>

**Cyclone Blade**<br>
Sneak+Use to summon a thunderstorm<br>
Use to hurl a cyclone burst<br>

**Seaglass Blade**<br>
Deals bonus damage to guardians<br>
Chance to inflict mining fatigue<br>

**Whisperer**<br>
Chance to wither foes<br>

**Fluorescent Zapper**<br>
Use to fire a zapping beam<br>
25% chance to set targets ablaze<br>

**Ionized Whipblade**<br>
Right-click to dash forward<br>
Kill grants bonus experience<br>

**Forged Blade**<br>
Use to sling molten metal<br>
33% chance to set targets ablaze<br>

**Bloodrose Blade**<br>
Deals bonus damage to undead mobs<br>
Grants regeneration when held<br>

**Violet Electrocutor**<br>
Use to fire a violet beam<br>

**Acid Edge**<br>
Chance to poison targets<br>
When it rains, use to spit acid<br>

**UV Triblade**<br>
Deals bonus damage to charged creepers<br>
1/5 chance to set targets ablaze<br>

**Charged Greatblade**<br>
Use to fire a charged bolt<br>
Charge creepers on hit<br>
Sets nearby foes ablaze when held<br>

**Mauve Taupe Sword**<br>
Chance to slow targets<br>
Use to fling a shard<br>

**Fossilized Dagger**<br>
Deals bonus damage to skeletons<br>
Doubles as bone meal<br>
Interact with wolves to tame them<br>
